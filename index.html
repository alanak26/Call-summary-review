Call Summary Review

QA check of collections-call summaries against transcripts

Workflow. Pick a call from the queue. Read the summary next to the transcript. Every flagged claim links to the transcript turns that support the finding. For each finding, choose Agree or Disagree, then set a verdict for the summary. If it needs correcting, edit the suggested corrected summary; the automated rule checks re-run as you type. Your decisions are saved in this browser. Export them as JSON or CSV to hand off.

Two sources of findings. Analyst findings are a pre-computed expert review of each call against the summary rules. They cover meaning, such as contradictions, unsupported claims and omissions. Automated checks are deterministic rules that run live on any summary text. They catch rule breaches and some factual mismatches, can produce false positives, and cannot judge meaning.

Severity reflects how likely the summary is to mislead a colleague. High: a wrong commitment, payment, permission, consent, hold or call direction. Medium: required compliance, hardship or cause details missing, or a privacy or money rule breached. Low: wording precision. Certainty separates definite errors from judgement calls.

Keyboard: j/k next/previous call, a accept, c needs correction.
